How To Deposit BGCash

Q : How much of a minimal deposit can I make using BGCash
A : Hello, we have a minimum limit of 100 rupees for a single deposit and a maximum limit of 50000 rupees for a single deposit.

66 Lottery have special provide payment method by using BGCash and you can follow the step by step of how to make the deposit below :
BGCash recharge method :
  • 1.Go to Account menu
  • 2.Choose Deposit button
  • 3.Select BGCash payment method
  • 4.Fill the Deposit Amount as you willing to deposit
  • 5.Entering the BGCash payment method page
  • 6.Please enter your payment password and click "Confirm Payment" button
  • 7.Click the "Confirm Recharge" button to confirm payment successful

    • There is some important thing you should aware when you made deposit for avoiding funds lost or deposit arrived late in your ID account :
    In order for the payment to be credited to your game account, we recommend that you remember to click the confirmation button.
