A : Our customer support are not able to modify or edit your bank account number information, but we only can assist member for delete the bank account that they bound in their ID account and after the deletion complete member will be able to add a new bank card that they want to use for future withdraw address.

Q : What is the purpose of the Delete Bank Account on self-service center 66 Lottery?
A: This Delete Bank Account purpose is for member who provide a wrong bank information such as the wrong 1-2 digit bank account number or the member who willing to change their previous bound bank to the new bank.

Q : How i can submit issue about Delete Bank Account on self-service center 66 Lottery?
A: To submit Delete Bank Account on self-service center 66 Lottery, please follow this step

  • 1.Login to your 66 Lottery ID Account
  • 2.Click Customer Service button
  • 3.Select issue and choose Delete Bank Account
  • 4.Fill your 66 Lottery ID accounts
  • 5.Attach photo selfie holding identity card clear and detail
  • 6.Attach photo of your passbook bank / bank statement clear and detail
  • 7.Attach photo of recent deposit receipt proof
  • 8.Click the "Confirm" button
