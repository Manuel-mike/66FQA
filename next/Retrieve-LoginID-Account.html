Retrieve Login ID Account

66 Lottery uses mobile phone numbers as login accounts. If you fill in the wrong mobile phone number to register, please submit it here!

Q : How do I submit a question about retrieving my login ID at the 66 Lottery Self-Service Center?
A : Submit the login ID account retrieval at the 66 Lottery Self-Service Center. Please follow the steps below.

After Login
  • 1.Visit our 66 Lottery official link at: https://www.66lottery.vip/
  • 2.Click Customer Service button
  • 3.Select the question and fill in your mobile phone number (search for reply information based on your mobile phone number)
  • 4.Fill in the mobile phone number bound to your 66 Lottery account to withdraw cash
  • 5.Fill in the bank account number bound to your 66 Lottery account to withdraw cash
  • 6.Fill in the IFSC code bank bound to your 66 Lottery account to withdraw cash
  • 7.Fill in the withdrawal email address bound to your 66 Lottery account
  • 8.Submit Issue
