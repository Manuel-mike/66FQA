Bank Name Change

Q : What will happen to my bound bank information if I submit Bank Name Change on self-service center 66 Lottery?
A : Our system will make a checking and verification to the information that you provide. If all the information is correct, our system will update the bank name that you are willing to change to the correct bank name.

Q : How can I submit issue about Bank Change Name on self-service center 66 Lottery?
A : What you need to do now is submit it on self-service center 66 Lottery and follow this guide to submit below :
  • 1.Login into your 66 Lottery ID Account
  • 2.Click the Customer Service button
  • 3.Select issue and choose "Change Bank Name"
  • 4.Fill in the correct bank account number that you bound in your ID account
  • 5.Choose the correct bank name following on the passbook bank / bank statement
  • 6.Click the "Confirm" button


    • Q : What i need to do after the Bank Name Change completed?
    A : After the Bank Name Change completed, your bound bank information will be changed to the correct bank name that you submit on the self-service center.
