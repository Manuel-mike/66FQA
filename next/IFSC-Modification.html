IFSC Modification

Q : What will happen to my bound bank information if i submit IFSC Modification on self-service center 66 Lottery?
A : Our system will conduct a checking and verification to the information that you provide and If all the information is correct. Our system will update the IFSC code that you willing to change to the correct IFSC code.

Q : How i can submit issue about IFSC Modification on self-service center 66 Lottery?
A : What you need to do now is submit it on self-service center 66 Lottery and follow this guide to submit it :

  • 1.Login to your 66 Lottery ID Account
  • 2.Click Customer Service button
  • 3.Choose IFSC Modification
  • 4.Choose Can Receive
  • 5.Fill correct bank account number that you bound in your ID account
  • 6.Fill correct IFSC bank code that you willing to change
  • 7.Click the "Confirm" button
