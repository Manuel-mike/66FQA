Delete Old USDT Address and Rebind

Q : How i can submit issue about Delete Old USDT Address and Rebind on self-service center 66 Lottery?
A :If you want to delete your USDT address information, we have two way for modify it that we will separate as "Can Receive" and "Cannot Receive" by the following step :
Can Receive
  • 1.Login to your 66 Lottery ID Account
  • 2.Click Customer Service button
  • 3.Choose Delete Old USDT and Rebind
  • 4.Choose Can Receive
  • 5.Click Sendand a verification code will be sent to your registered phone number
  • 6.After receiving the code, fill the verification code
  • 7.Click the "Confirm" button
    • Cannot Receive
  • 1.Login to your 66 Lottery ID Account
  • 2.Click Customer Service button
  • 3.Choose Delete Old USDT and Rebind
  • 4.Fill your 66 Lottery ID account
  • 5.Attach photo selfie holding identity card clear and detail
  • 6.Attach photo selfie holding USDT address clear and detail
  • 7.Attach deposit receipt proof clear and detail
  • 8.Click the "Confirm" button

    • Q : What i need to do after the Delete USDT Address have been completed ?
    A :After the USDT Address deletion completed, member can add a new USDT address or make a correction to the previous address information that they bound in their ID account by go to "Withdraw" page and following the steps below :

  • 1.Click add a USDT
  • 2.Choose the correct main network
  • 3.Fill in the correct USDT Address and Re-Fill the correct USDT Address in "Address Alias"
  • 4.Click Save and SMS Verification menu will appear
  • 5.Click the Send button
  • 6.Fill the correct OTP Code
  • 7.Click the "Add Confirm" button
