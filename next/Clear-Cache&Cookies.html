Clear Cache & Cookies

When you use a browser, like Chrome, it saves some information from websites in its cache and cookies. Clearing them fixes certain problems, like loading or formatting issues on sites.

A. Computer
In Chrome
  • 1.On your computer, open Chrome.
  • 2.At the top right, click More then Delete browsing data.
  • 3.Choose a time range, like Last hour or All time.
  • 4.Select the types of information you want to remove.
  • 5.Click Delete data button
    • If you delete cookies while signed in to Chrome, you won't be signed out of your Google Account.

    Tips
  • 1.To sign out of your Google Account on all websites, sign out of Chrome.
  • 2.In the address bar, to quickly reach the Delete browsing data dialog, type "Delete browsing data" and then, tap the Action chip.

    • In other browsers
  • 1.If you use Safari, Firefox, or another browser, check its support site for instructions.

    • What happens after you clear this info
    After you clear cache and cookies :
  • 1.Some settings on sites get deleted. For example, if you were signed in, you'll need to sign in again.
  • 2.If you turn sync on in Chrome, you’ll stay signed into the Google Account you're syncing to in order to delete your data across all your devices.
  • 3.Some sites can seem slower because content, like images, needs to load again.

    • How cache & cookies work
  • 1.Cookies are files created by sites you visit. They make your online experience easier by saving browsing data.
  • 2.The cache remembers parts of pages, like images, to help them open faster during your next visit.

    When you clear your cache and cookies, your browser might sign you out of your Google Account. To make sure you can sign back in, update your recovery phone number and email address.

  • 5.To select specific data you want to delete :
    • a. Tap Browsing Data.
    b. Select the types of browsing data you want to delete.
